# Macro Analysis & Regime Detection

Macro drivers evaluated in `src/metals/` and `src/regimes/`:
- **Real Yields & Rates**: Impact of US 10Y Real Yields and Nominal Yields on non-yielding metals.
- **US Dollar Index (DXY)**: Inverse currency valuation relationship.
- **Volatilities**: VIX and India VIX risk appetite signals.
- **Macro Regimes**: Inflationary, Disinflationary, Tightening, Easing, Risk-Off, and Neutral based on real yields, DXY returns, and equity market volatility.
