# Macro Analysis Framework

The macro engine evaluates multi-asset interactions across:

- **US Dollar Index (DXY)**
- **Treasury Yields (10Y, 2Y) & Real Yields**
- **Crude Oil & Energy Markets**
- **Global & Indian Equities (S&P 500, NIFTY 50)**
- **Equity Volatility (VIX, India VIX)**

## Macro Regimes

- **Inflationary**: High oil price momentum / rising yields.
- **Stagflationary**: High inflation drivers with falling equities.
- **Risk-Off**: Elevated VIX (> 22) or equity drawdowns.
- **Risk-On**: Subdued VIX (< 16) with rising equities.
- **Tightening / Easing**: Coordinated yield and USD direction.
