# Macro Analysis & Regime Classification

## Macro Relationships
Evaluates rolling multi-horizon cross-market correlations (20d, 60d, 120d, 252d) between metals and:
- DXY Index
- US Rates & Real Yields
- Crude Oil
- Equities (S&P 500, NIFTY 50)
- Volatility (VIX, India VIX)

## Macro Regimes
Rule-based classification combining rate trends, dollar strength, inflation, and market sentiment:
- Inflationary
- Disinflationary
- Risk-On / Risk-Off
- Tightening / Easing
- Stagflationary
- Neutral
