# Macro Analysis Specifications

## Macro Factor Dynamics

Gold and Silver dynamics depend on cross-asset relationships:
- **US Dollar Index (DXY):** Inverse relationship with USD-denominated metal prices.
- **US 10Y/2Y Treasury Yields & Real Yields:** Opportunity cost drivers for non-yielding bullion.
- **Crude Oil & Energy:** Leading indicator for headline inflation expectations.
- **S&P 500 & NIFTY 50:** Risk appetite sentiment indicators.
- **VIX & India VIX:** Market anxiety and safe-haven demand gauges.

## Rolling Correlations

The system computes 20-day, 60-day, and 252-day rolling correlation matrices between Gold and DXY, US10Y, Oil, and Equities to detect correlation breakdown or regime shifts.
