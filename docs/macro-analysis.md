# Macro Analysis & Regime Detection

## Macro Drivers
- **US Dollar Index (DXY)**: Inversely correlated with precious metals during standard monetary regimes.
- **US Treasury Yields & Real Yields (TIP)**: Real yields represent the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding precious metals.
- **Inflation Metrics**: Measured via CPI, PCE, and commodity proxies (Crude Oil).
- **Equity Markets & Volatility**: S&P 500, NIFTY 50, VIX, India VIX as risk sentiment proxies.

## Macro Regimes
The system classifies macro environment into rule-based regimes:
- **Inflationary**
- **Disinflationary**
- **Deflationary**
- **Risk-on**
- **Risk-off**
- **Tightening**
- **Easing**
- **Stagflationary**
- **Neutral**

Rolling cross-market correlations are calculated over 20, 60, 120, and 252 trading days.
