# Macro Regime & Correlation Analysis

Precious metals exhibit dynamic relationships with interest rates, currency valuation, equity sentiment, and inflation expectations.

## 1. Multi-Window Rolling Correlations

Calculated across 20D, 60D, 120D, and 252D rolling windows between metals returns and:
- **US Dollar Index (DXY)**: Inverse correlation during standard monetary regimes.
- **US Treasury Yields (10Y, 2Y)**: Real yield impact on non-yielding metals.
- **Crude Oil (WTI/Brent)**: Energy-driven inflation proxy.
- **Equity Indices (S&P 500, NIFTY 50)**: Risk sentiment indicators.
- **Volatility Indices (VIX, India VIX)**: Systemic risk proxies.

## 2. Macro Regime Classification Matrix

| Primary Regime | Condition Criteria | Typical Gold Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Risk-Off** | High Volatility ($\text{VIX} > 25$) OR Equity Trend Down | Strong Support (Safe Haven) |
| **Inflationary** | Crude Oil Rising AND Interest Rates Rising | Highly Supportive |
| **Stagflationary** | Crude Oil Rising AND Equities Falling | Extremely Supportive |
| **Easing** | Interest Rates Falling | Supportive |
| **Tightening** | Interest Rates Rising | Headwind |
| **Risk-On** | Equities Rising AND Low Volatility ($\text{VIX} < 15$) | Moderate Headwind |
