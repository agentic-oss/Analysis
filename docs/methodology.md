# Research & Forecasting Methodology

## Core Philosophy

All predictions generated by this pipeline are explicitly framed as **historical conditional research statistics**, not guaranteed future outcomes.

## Feature Store & Temporal Integrity

- Features at time $T$ are strictly constructed using information known on or before $T$.
- Target variables (`future_return_1d`, `future_return_5d`, etc.) are computed by looking strictly into the future ($T+h$).
- Cross-validation uses walk-forward expanding window splits to guarantee zero data leakage or look-ahead bias.

## Confidence Framework

Confidence scores (0–100) are evaluated based on:
1. Historical sample size of top analogue matches.
2. Model consensus agreement across classifier and regressor predictions.
3. Feature stability and current regime volatility.
