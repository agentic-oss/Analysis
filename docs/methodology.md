# Research Methodology & Philosophy

The objective of this pipeline is to establish a rigorous, objective research database for precious metals without hype or ungrounded assertions.

## Core Rules

1. **Probabilistic Research Signals**: All forecasts are expressed as historical conditional statistics and probabilities.
2. **Temporal Correctness**: Date T features must only contain information available up to date T.
3. **Multi-Factor Context**: Models combine technical momentum, macro drivers, cross-market correlations, relative-value ratios, and historical analogue behavior.
