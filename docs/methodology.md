# Methodology & Philosophical Framework

## Research Philosophy
This pipeline is designed for objective quantitative market intelligence. Every signal generated by the platform is framed strictly as a **probabilistic research signal** derived from historical conditional distributions, multi-factor similarity matching, and walk-forward backtests.

## Anti-Look-Ahead Bias Guarantees
1. Features for observation date $T$ only utilize data available up to timestamp $T$.
2. Target variables for future returns ($T + h$) are stored separately or explicitly prefixed (`future_return_5d`).
3. Historical analogue searches restrict the pool strictly to dates $< T - \text{max\_horizon}$.
4. Strategy backtests lag signals by 1 day to execute at next-day close prices.
