# Research & Analytical Methodology

## Core Principles
1. **Probabilistic Research Signals**: All system outputs express historical conditional probabilities rather than absolute or guaranteed price predictions.
2. **Temporal Correctness & Zero Look-Ahead Bias**: Features computed on date T only utilize data available on or before date T.
3. **Multi-Factor Synergy**: Research biases are formed by combining technical indicators, cross-asset correlations, macroeconomic drivers, and relative value metrics.
4. **Transparent & Explainable Scoring**: Black-box scores are strictly avoided. Every composite score details its exact component pillar contributions.
