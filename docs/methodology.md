# Research Methodology

## Principles

1. **Probabilistic Research vs. Predictions:** The platform provides conditional historical statistics and probabilistic research signals rather than deterministic "guaranteed" price targets.
2. **Strict Temporal Separation:** All feature calculations and analogue matches on date T strictly restrict data availability to dates $\le T$ to prevent look-ahead bias.
3. **Multi-Factor Explanations:** Numerical scores (0-100) are fully transparent and documented with component factor breakdowns.
4. **Indian-Market First Class Status:** Indian gold and silver spot measurements (INR/10g and INR/kg) incorporate exact USD/INR exchange rates and customs duty parameters.
