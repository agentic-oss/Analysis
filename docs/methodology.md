# Research & Forecasting Methodology

## Probabilistic Signal Philosophy

All signals generated by this system represent **historical conditional research statistics**, not guaranteed future price movements.

### Factor Scores (0-100)
1. **Technical Score**: Evaluates price position relative to 20, 50, and 200 SMAs.
2. **Momentum Score**: Evaluates RSI (14) and MACD histogram momentum.
3. **Volatility Score**: Assesses 20-day annualized volatility relative to historical mean.
4. **Macro Score**: Maps macro regime (Stagflationary, Risk-off, Inflationary, Tightening) to historical precious metals behavior.
5. **Relative Value Score**: Evaluates Gold/Silver ratio Z-score and mean-reversion setups.
6. **Historical Pattern Score**: Calculated from positive forward-return probability among top historical analogues.

### Historical Analogue Engine
When observing Date T, the system normalizes feature vectors (RSI, MACD, MA Distances, Volatility, Ratio) using historical z-score standardization up to Date T-1. Euclidean distance is computed against historical observations to identify top analogues and extract 1D, 3D, 5D, 10D, 20D, and 60D forward return distributions.

### Temporal Correctness & Look-Ahead Protection
Target columns (`future_return_1d`, `future_return_5d`, etc.) are explicitly shifted into the future and stored separately. Feature calculations for Date T strictly restrict inputs to information available on or before Date T.
