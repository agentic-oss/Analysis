# Research Methodology

## Research Philosophy

The primary objective of this platform is **rigorous historical research and probabilistic analysis**, rather than sensational market predictions.

All output signals follow the research pipeline:

`Observed Market Data ──► Technical & Macro Indicators ──► Historical State Vectors ──► Analogue Pattern Matching & ML Models ──► Probabilistic Forward Research Signal`

## Probabilistic Signal Guidelines

- **No Guarantees**: Model predictions are expressed strictly as probabilistic conditional research signals (e.g. "Historical analogues under similar conditions yielded positive returns 64% of the time").
- **Look-Ahead Protection**: All calculations for date $T$ strictly restrict feature inputs to information available on or before date $T$. Historical analogue search excludes the most recent $N$ days to avoid overlap and leakage.
- **Explainable Scoring**: All 0-100 sub-scores (Technical, Macro, Momentum, Volatility, Relative Value, Pattern) record their individual component values and formulas.
