# Probabilistic Forecasting Framework

All forward-looking signals are framed as **historical conditional research signals**:

- Signals evaluate multi-horizon probabilities (1D, 3D, 5D, 10D, 20D, 60D).
- Probabilities represent the empirical frequency of positive returns among nearest multi-factor historical analogues adjusted by transparent composite score sub-components.
- Outcomes are stored on date T alongside predicted signals for continuous post-hoc accuracy tracking.
