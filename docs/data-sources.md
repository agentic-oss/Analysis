# Data Sources & Instruments

Configured in `config/instruments.json`:

- **Precious Metals**: Spot Gold (`GC=F`), Spot Silver (`SI=F`), Indian Gold ETF (`GOLDBEES.NS`), Indian Silver ETF (`SILVERBEES.NS`).
- **Currencies**: USD/INR (`INR=X`), US Dollar Index (`DX-Y.NYB`).
- **Rates**: US 10-Year Yield (`^TNX`), US 2-Year Yield (`^IRX`).
- **Commodities**: Crude Oil (`CL=F`), Brent Crude (`BZ=F`).
- **Equities**: S&P 500 (`^GSPC`), Nasdaq (`^IXIC`), Dow Jones (`^DJI`), NIFTY 50 (`^NSEI`).
- **Volatility**: CBOE VIX (`^VIX`), India VIX (`^INDIAVIX`).

All observations record source provider, instrument, market, currency, unit, and retrieval timestamp.
