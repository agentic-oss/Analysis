# Data Sources

## Configured Instruments (`config/instruments.json`)

### Precious Metals
- **Spot Gold (USD/oz)**: Ticker `GC=F`
- **Spot Silver (USD/oz)**: Ticker `SI=F`
- **Gold Futures (USD/oz)**: Ticker `GC=F`
- **Silver Futures (USD/oz)**: Ticker `SI=F`
- **Indian Gold ETF (INR/10g)**: Ticker `GOLDBEES.NS`
- **Indian Silver ETF (INR/kg)**: Ticker `SILVERBEES.NS`

### Currencies
- **USD/INR FX Rate**: Ticker `USDINR=X`
- **US Dollar Index (DXY)**: Ticker `DX-Y.NYB`

### Rates & Yields
- **US 10Y Treasury Yield**: Ticker `^TNX`
- **US 2Y Treasury Yield**: Ticker `^IRX`
- **TIPS Real Yield ETF**: Ticker `TIP`

### Commodities & Energy
- **Brent Crude Oil**: Ticker `BZ=F`
- **WTI Crude Oil**: Ticker `CL=F`

### Equity & Volatility Indices
- **S&P 500**: Ticker `^GSPC`
- **Nasdaq Composite**: Ticker `^IXIC`
- **Dow Jones Industrial**: Ticker `^DJI`
- **NIFTY 50**: Ticker `^NSEI`
- **CBOE Volatility Index (VIX)**: Ticker `^VIX`
- **India VIX**: Ticker `^INDIAVIX`
