# Data Sources Specification

- **Precious Metals (Spot & Futures):** GC=F (COMEX Gold Futures), SI=F (COMEX Silver Futures), GOLDBEES.NS (Nippon India ETF Gold BeES), SILVERBEES.NS (Nippon India ETF Silver BeES).
- **Currencies & FX:** USDINR=X (FOREX USD/INR rate), DX-Y.NYB (ICE US Dollar Index).
- **Interest Rates & Bonds:** ^TNX (US 10-Year Treasury Yield), ^IRX (US Short Rate Proxy), TIP (iShares TIPS Bond ETF / Real Yield Proxy).
- **Energy:** CL=F (WTI Crude Oil Futures), BZ=F (Brent Crude Oil Futures).
- **Equities & Volatility:** ^GSPC (S&P 500), ^IXIC (Nasdaq Composite), ^DJI (Dow Jones), ^NSEI (NIFTY 50), ^VIX (CBOE Volatility Index), ^INDIAVIX (India Volatility Index).
