# Data Sources & Configuration

Data sources and instrument tickers are configured via `config/instruments.json` and `config/config.yaml`.

Supported instruments:
- Precious Metals: Gold Spot (GC=F), Silver Spot (SI=F), MCX Gold (GOLDBEES.NS), MCX Silver (SILVERBEES.NS)
- Currencies: USD/INR (INR=X), DXY Dollar Index (DX-Y.NYB)
- Rates: US 10Y (^TNX), US 2Y (^IRX), US Real Yields (TIP)
- Energy & Commodities: Brent Crude (BZ=F), WTI (CL=F), Copper (HG=F)
- Equities: S&P 500 (^GSPC), Nasdaq (^IXIC), Dow Jones (^DJI), NIFTY 50 (^NSEI)
- Volatility: VIX (^VIX), India VIX (^INDIAVIX)
