# Technical Analysis Methodology

## Calculated Technical Indicators

1. **Moving Averages**:
   - Simple Moving Averages: SMA 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200
   - Exponential Moving Averages: EMA 9, 20, 50, 200

2. **Momentum Indicators**:
   - Relative Strength Index (RSI 14)
   - Stochastic RSI
   - MACD (12, 26, 9) with signal line and histogram
   - Rate of Change (ROC 10) & Momentum

3. **Volatility Indicators**:
   - Average True Range (ATR 14)
   - Annualized 20-Day Historical Volatility
   - Bollinger Bands (20-period SMA, 2.0 std deviations) & Bandwidth

4. **Volume Indicators**:
   - Volume SMA 20 & Relative Volume
   - On-Balance Volume (OBV)

5. **Structural & Pattern Detection**:
   - Distance from SMAs (20, 50, 200)
   - Distance from 52-week High and Low
   - Golden Cross & Death Cross flags
   - 20-day Breakout and Breakdown signals
