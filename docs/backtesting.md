# Backtesting Engine

## Core Features
- Signal-based strategy simulation.
- Costs & Friction: Transaction costs (e.g. 10 bps) and slippage (e.g. 5 bps).
- Position sizing: Dynamic capital allocation with risk parameters.
- Look-ahead bias prevention: Signals on day T-1 trigger execution on day T open/close.

## Performance Metrics Reported
- Cumulative Return (%)
- Annualized Return (%)
- Maximum Drawdown (%)
- Sharpe Ratio & Sortino Ratio
- Win Rate (%) & Profit Factor
- Total Trades & Costs Paid
